About roles

Owner: one person, who can do everything, including making and removing admins. They can hand ownership to someone else.
Admins: edit the plan, approve changes from Ask AI, see everyone’s ticks and answers, invite members and make password-reset links.
Members: see the plan, tick things off, and manage their own answers, progress and chats.
