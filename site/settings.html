Where you shop

Pick one or more. The shopping list shows a search link for each, and Ask Claude knows where you shop. Plan prices are Coles estimates, so other stores will differ a little.

Your stores

The household’s stores (admins): used by anyone who hasn’t picked their own

Your Claude key

Ask Claude normally uses the family’s key (if an admin has set one up). You can use your own instead: then you pay Anthropic for your own messages and the daily limit doesn’t apply. Make a key at console.anthropic.com (Claude.ai subscriptions can’t be used by other apps, so it needs an API key).

The key is checked with Anthropic, then stored encrypted on your family’s server. It’s never shown again in full, and only used for your own chats.
