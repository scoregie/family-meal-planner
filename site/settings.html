How energy is shown

How energy is shown in Meals (Macros), on Today and the Progress page, and in Ask AI’s answers. Australian food labels use kilojoules; 1 Calorie (kcal) is about 4.2 kJ.

Your AI key

Use your own Claude, OpenAI or Gemini key

Ask AI normally uses the family’s AI (an admin chooses it on Plan setup). You can use your own instead, from Claude, OpenAI or Google Gemini: then you pay for your own messages and the daily limit doesn’t apply. Chat subscriptions (Claude Pro, ChatGPT Plus, Gemini Advanced) can’t be used by other apps, so it needs an API key.

Which AI

The key is checked with the company, then stored encrypted on your family’s server. It’s never shown again in full, and only used for your own chats and any plan you draft.
