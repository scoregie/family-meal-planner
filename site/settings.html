Your AI key

Use your own Claude, OpenAI or Gemini key

Add your own key here and it writes the plans you draft and answers your Ask AI. Without one, Ask AI uses the family’s key on the server, if there is one. Keys come from Claude, OpenAI or Google Gemini: you pay for your own use, and the daily limit doesn’t apply. Chat subscriptions (Claude Pro, ChatGPT Plus, Gemini Advanced) can’t be used by other apps, so it needs an API key.

Which AI

The key is checked with the company, then stored encrypted on your family’s server. It’s never shown again in full, and only used for your own chats and any plan you draft.
