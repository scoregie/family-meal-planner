Exercise library

The exercises the app’s plan builder chooses from

← Back

Each one says what it needs and what it loads most. When you build a plan without AI, workouts are picked from here to suit your equipment and what you said in About me. To change an exercise in your plan, tap Swap next to it on the Exercise tab.

This is general information, not medical advice. If you have an injury, a health condition or are pregnant, consider checking with your GP or physio which exercises suit you, and stop anything that causes pain.
