1Everyone’s About me answers

Each person answers the About me questions and taps “I’m finished”. For someone without a login (like a young child), you can answer for them.

Add someone without an email (like a child)

Usernames must be different from everyone else’s. Tell them their username and password; they log in with those instead of an email. You can make a password-reset link for them on the Family page.

2Your AI

An AI fits the plan closely to everyone’s answers. Without one, the app’s own plan builder makes the plan for free.

What’s already in your kitchen?

The family lists the food at home on the Current food page, sorted by where it is kept (pantry, spices and seasonings, fridge, freezer, fruit bowl and bench, other). The next plan you draft prefers meals that use it and leaves what it covers off the shopping lists.
